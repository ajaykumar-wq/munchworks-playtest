    Before you play

    This is a test of two unfinished puzzle games made by IICT. You can play without sending anything. If you choose to send your answers, here is exactly what happens.

    What is sent, only when you press Send

    • Your answers to the questions on this page.
    • Your level stats for each game: highest level reached, levels won, and for each level the time played, fails, runs, retries and hints.
    • Your device type, read from your browser's user agent: for example Android phone · Chrome · 393×873.
    • A random tester ID made on this device (yours: ), the age option you choose below, and the game build.

    Not sent: your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or anything else on your device.

    Where it goes and why

    To a Google Form owned by IICT. Google runs the form, so, like any website, it also sees your internet (IP) address. We use the answers only to find out whether players understand the new game, how far they get and which game they prefer, so we can improve the design. They are never used for ads or sold.

    We keep them for , then delete them.

    Your rights (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023)
    • You can change your mind at any time: open "Privacy and age" at the bottom of the page and choose "Just play".
    • To see, correct or delete what you sent, or to complain, write to with your tester ID. If we don't resolve it, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
    • "Forget everything on this browser" deletes your progress and stats from this browser.
